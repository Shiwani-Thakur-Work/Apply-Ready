ApplyReady

From gap analysis to a ready-to-send resume.

ApplyReady started as a simple idea: instead of manually comparing a resume against a job description, let AI point out exactly what's missing — specific keywords, weak bullet points, and an honest fit score.

It's grown into a full loop: upload a resume, see the gaps, generate an improved version, review it, and download it as a PDF or Word document — all in one place.

Few more features will be added soon :)
